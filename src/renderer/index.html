Ticker Screening

Screen tickers for significant recent price events.

Re-ranks the screening universe (~910 US equities > $2B cap, > 2M avg volume, via Yahoo screener + curated list held to the same thresholds) on two independent criteria — growth (revenue/equity growth × leverage) and event-proneness (beta / 52-week range). Does not change your screening list — each saved-list run additionally draws 5 random tickers from the ranking's top 100 (marked "shuffle" in the results, with an Add button to keep them).
